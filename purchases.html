Loader Land: purchases, refunds and privacy

Everything in Loader Land can be played for free. You can also buy premium skins and gems.

What you can buy

  • Premium skins ($1.99 to $4.99): special looks for your loaders, with their own effects. Each is bought once and is yours to keep.
  • Gems (80 for $0.99, 500 for $4.99, 1,200 for $9.99, 2,800 for $19.99): used to finish a mine build straight away. Gems are used up when you spend them.

Prices are in US dollars. Sales tax or VAT is added where it applies, and shown at checkout before you pay.

Paying

Payments are handled by Stripe, by card, Apple Pay or Google Pay. We never see or store your card details.

Getting your skins on another device

In the game's paint shop, use Restore with the email you used at checkout, or with the restore code shown after you bought something (it's also kept in the game's gem shop). Gems stay on the device you bought them on and can't be restored, because they're used up.

Refunds and help

If something went wrong with a purchase (it didn't arrive, or you were charged twice), contact us and we'll put it right, including a refund where it's due.

Privacy

  • Your game progress is saved on your device, not on our servers.
  • When you buy something, Stripe handles your payment details, email and billing address (needed for tax) under Stripe's privacy policy.
  • Our small shop server keeps your Stripe customer number with your restore code, and which purchases have been handed to which device (a random id your browser keeps). It also counts restore attempts from each internet address for up to two hours, to stop guessing.
  • We don't sell your data or show ads.
